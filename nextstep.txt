7. CRAFT MAKER + FOLD ANIMATION PLAYER - done 2026-09-03

The animation spike is finished and the feature is built. The spike's actual
hard question was NOT the file format, it was how to represent paper once it
stops being one flat shape. The answer that shipped:

  Paper after N folds is an ORDERED STACK OF CONVEX POLYGONS, bottom first.
  A fold cuts every layer along the fold line, reflects the pieces on the
  moving side across it, and re-stacks those as a group IN REVERSE ORDER.
  Valley drops the group on top, mountain slides it underneath.

  It works because a rectangle is convex and both half-plane clipping and
  reflection preserve convexity, so every layer stays convex forever. That
  makes the whole engine a Sutherland-Hodgman clip plus a cosine - no
  polygon-boolean library, no physics. Area is conserved exactly.

  Animation: seen from overhead, a flap rotating out of plane only narrows by
  cos(theta), so it closes to a crease at the halfway point and opens out
  mirrored. GSAP tweens that one scalar; the paths are rebuilt per frame.

WHAT IT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO (do not treat these as bugs):
  - no paper thickness, no layers trapped inside a pocket
  - no curved folds
  - no TRUE reverse/squash/petal folds, which move part of a flap THROUGH the
    stack rather than over it. Steps typed reverse/squash/petal still animate,
    as the straight fold their line describes.
  - layers capped at 96; real tutorials sit in the low tens

TWO GOTCHAS WORTH KNOWING, both about folds that are geometrically correct
and still look wrong:

  1. A fold line that does not cross the shape the previous fold left behind
     is not an error anywhere - it folds nothing, and the step plays as a
     dead frame.
  2. A fold that halves the shape EXACTLY covers the stationary half, so the
     flap's back face is all you see and the paper reads as one flat colour.
     Two-tone paint does not save you; the sequence has to leave both faces
     showing. Corner folds and offset edge folds do, exact halves do not.

The seeded sequence is checked against the replay engine for both, by
rasterising each step and counting which face is actually visible. Checking
area and layer counts alone does NOT catch #2.

THE FOLD TOOL IS GESTURE BASED - do not "simplify" it back to drawing creases.
Click 1 is the point of the paper that MOVES, click 2 is where it LANDS. The
crease is the perpendicular bisector of that pair and the moving half is the
half click 1 sits in, so both are DERIVED, never chosen. Fold type and which
half travels are picked explicitly after that, and nothing is written to the
step list until Record. The earlier version had you draw the crease directly
and then defaulted side to left and foldType to valley: that made you do the
bisector in your head, and the two defaults were right about half the time,
which is what made the maker look like it was guessing every fold.
CraftFoldStep.origin/target record the gesture. They are OPTIONAL because the
player reads only from/to/side, so files authored before this replay fine.
